The Distinction That Matters

AI Tools vs AI Integration

AI tools are standalone products people use manually. AI integration is embedding AI capabilities directly into your business systems — where they operate automatically, on your data, serving your customers, within your workflow.
AI Tools (Not What We Do)
Standalone products. Manual usage. Generic outputs. Not connected to your data.
AI Integration (What We Build)
Embedded. Automatic. Trained on your data. Connected to your CRM, website, and workflows.

Real Estate AI Integration Dubai, UAE · 30 Agents · 600–800 Inquiries/Month

Education / EdTech LMS Build Pan-India · 12,000 Students · Coaching Institute

Custom LMS for a Pan-India Competitive Exam Coaching Institute

Problem Statement

12,000 students with content on public YouTube (no access control), PDFs via WhatsApp, and Zoom with no recording management. No student dashboard, no assessment system, no progress tracking, no way to measure learning outcomes. Content shared externally with no recourse.

System Architecture

Custom LMS on Node.js + React, deployed on AWS with CloudFront CDN. Video delivered via Bunny.net with signed URL expiry. Hierarchical course structure with access control. Timed assessment module. WhatsApp API for reminders. Razorpay for enrollment.
